# Contributing

Changes are welcome, especially updates that track Brave's official policy reference.

Please keep the project:

- explicit that it is unofficial and not the Origin product;
- supportive of Brave's work and respectful of its licensing and trademarks;
- reversible and respectful of pre-existing managed policies;
- based on documented, stable policies rather than activation workarounds;
- free of credentials, binaries and remote execution.

Test install, verification and uninstall on the operating system affected by your change.
